# Why I made this

I'm a 2nd year ECE student at St. Aloysius in Mangalore. I needed a
project that wasn't just another line-following robot or basic IoT thing.

At my place, the internet keeps going down and nobody can ever say why.
Last time we opened the router adapter it was really hot and one
capacitor looked swollen. It made me think: can you tell a device is
getting sick just by watching how much current it pulls?

Like a heartbeat.

So that's the project. A small box that sits on the power line and
watches the electricity. No camera, no app on the device, nothing
inside. Just current and voltage.

Then I realized this works for anything with DC power. Security cameras,
medical fridges, industrial machines. And if I log everything with
cryptographic signatures, I can prove when a device failed due to
internal defects vs power issues. That's the warranty defense angle.

That's it. No big story. I just wanted to build something that watches
machines without touching them and gives you proof when they break.

Machine bachao ig.
